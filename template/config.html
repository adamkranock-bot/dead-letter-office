Dead Letter Office also follows Reddit Enhancement Suite's night mode. "Match system" switches with your operating system's light/dark setting.

By default, messages in a conversation are sorted from old to new. Showing the first message at the top and the last message at the bottom. This option allows you to reverse the sorting so that messages are sorted from new to old inside a message thread.

Dead Letter Office

Reddit moved direct messages to Chat and turned your old private messages into a read-only archive. Dead Letter Office keeps a copy of that archive in your browser, so you can search, sort, and export years of conversations. Nothing leaves your computer except requests to reddit.com.

Keyboard shortcuts

j/k next/previous conversation, Enter or o open, x select, / search, Esc or u back to the list.
